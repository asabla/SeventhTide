## Crew portraits and route chart

The boarding surface uses three circular crops from [crew-portraits.png](crew-portraits.png). The chart pairs [route-chart.png](route-chart.png) with selectable routes for the host and a read-only view for guests. The deck tool rail uses circular crops of the existing action illustrations.

**Crew portraits**

> Three completely independent square head-and-shoulders portraits arranged edge to edge in one exactly 3:1 horizontal triptych, three equal panels for mechanical slicing. Each face centered horizontally and vertically within its own square and readable at 64 pixels within a tight circular crop. For Seventh Tide, a late Victorian maritime folk-horror board game, faithful to a genuine copperplate engraving and aquatint on worn warm bone paper. Natural human faces, directly facing camera, dramatic engraved contours, restrained dark petrol-green shadows, warm lamplight, old saturated sepia. No ornament, name, frames, separators, typography, or borders. LEFT third: Mara, an authoritative woman boatswain with dark hair under a tarred knit cap, in her early 40s, a thick turned oilskin collar. MIDDLE third: Ivo, a weathered middle-aged shipwright with a salt-and-pepper short beard and square face, navy wool work cap and heavy canvas coat. RIGHT third: Nell, an alert young adult woman lookout with short wavy hair tucked under a rain hood and a narrow focused face; dark green oilskin. All three worn out, surviving on a wrecked 1890 steamer's raft. Quiet charcoal water and open tonal space around each head. Close portrait with clear face and ample breathing room. Full bleed.

**Route chart**

> One full-bleed antique nautical pilot chart detail for Seventh Tide, a realistic 1890 maritime folk-horror card game. Square plate. Overhead view straight down, no perspective, no cast objects. Contrast of pale worn honey-bone parchment and dark muted petrol-green ink in authentic Victorian copperplate engraving and aquatint. Depict a curving coast and broken flinty reef with parallel etched depth soundings and wave hatching, a tiny top-down raft upstream, and hand-drawn currents flowing around shoals. Top left a small serious antique ink compass rose with four long points and no letters; a few subtle dashed route trails on the water. They must evoke navigation through dark water; no map text, letters, numbering, symbols that resemble letters, ornamental border, hands, props, wax seal, annotations, or interface. Quiet sea around the perimeter for blending into the same parchment. Coarse paper texture, salt stains, irregular line weights, crisp engraved contours at small size. Color restricted to aged parchment and sea-green ink. Authentic functional working chart, mysterious coast.

## The second wake

The original [second-wake.png](second-wake.png) supplies the [sea banner](../web/assets/second-wake.webp). Phaser frames the swimmer and the wake. The animation places a moving shadow and a trace of foam over the engraving.

> Create a wide 3:2 landscape title-free scene for Seventh Tide, a late Victorian maritime folk-horror tabletop game. Museum-print antique copperplate engraving and aquatint, realistic anatomy, carbon-black etched lines, desaturated petrol green, worn bone, a trace of lantern amber. First-person view from a very low drifting timber raft at night. No deck in the foreground, only water and the thick taut tether leaving the bottom left edge of the frame and entering the water toward a tiny, fully clothed oilskin swimmer. The swimmer's dark cap and face just above water and the end of the taut hemp tether are in the middle of the image just RIGHT of its center (horizontal 58%, vertical 50%). Behind the swimmer an unnaturally distinct bright crescent wake moves against the softer parallel swell, starting at open water in the upper center and curling around toward the right side of the swimmer. Just beneath the foaming curl a gigantic dark shadow curves through the translucent water, an almost geological shape with a suggestion of ridges. No obvious eyes, mouth, recognizable animal, claws, tentacles above water, or gore. The silhouette of a half-sunk 1890 steamship remains tiny and far off on the hazy horizon. Use a quiet dark left third that can take a title over it in the game. Place the swimmer and uncanny leading part of the wake close in the middle so they can both be seen when cropped down to the middle third on a phone or to a shallow desktop banner. Chiaroscuro, wet hatch marks, tactile engraving and oppressively cold water. Full bleed. No lettering, numerals, letters, border, logos, panels, or interface.
