# Playing Seventh Tide

Keep the raft afloat and all three sailors warm until the end of tide seven. At tide resolution, the voyage ends if the raft has zero hull or any sailor has at least five cold.

## Start a voyage

- **Play solo** gives you Mara’s seat and two bot crewmates. The browser saves your voyage on the device.
- **Invite one friend** gives your friend Ivo’s seat, with Nell as a bot. **Invite two friends** gives each friend a seat. Share the private links in the crew lobby. A seat shows **Joined** when its player opens the link. You can start with open seats and friends can still join later.

Use the [local quick start](../README.md#run-locally) for solo play or [start the multiplayer server](hosting.md) to play with friends.

## Play a tide

1. **Choose a swimmer.** Each player can volunteer their sailor. The helm can choose a volunteer or a bot. With no volunteers, the helm can assign any sailor. The boarding preview shows normal exposure before equipment and the encounter change the outcome.
2. **Make your play.** Each sailor can use a card or take a crew action. Humans can play in any order. Bots act after the human turns.
3. **Resolve the tide.** Each route forecasts the resulting hull and swimmer cold. A warning names any sailor who will reach fatal cold after recovery. Guests can recommend a route by selecting it. Select it again to withdraw the recommendation. The helm commits the route.

At the start of the voyage, the helm chooses one salvage option. The ration locker adds Pass the Tin, spare timber adds Emergency Patch, and dry canvas adds Canvas Sling. Two copies of that card join the shared deck of 23 cards.

Each sailor holds two equipment cards. A card can have different effects on the raft and in the water. During boarding, open a card to compare both effects. To play it, drag it to the tray or select the card and activate the tray. A sailor must have a ration to play a face that spends one. Playing equipment discards it and draws a replacement. Crew actions such as **Lash the timbers** and **Share a ration** use your turn and keep both cards. The crew reshuffles the discard pile when the draw pile runs out.

Each player sees their own hand and the crew’s public plays. During card play, open **Signals** to ask for warmth or brace, or offer a ration. Select an active flag again to lower it. The Ship’s log shows the crew’s supplies, condition, and active flags. Signals and route recommendations clear when the tide resolves.

Open **Rules** to look up the encounter rules for your voyage. Open **Entries** to read the crew’s previous plays and tide results.

## Keep the crew together

If a friend is away, the helm can wait or let a bot cover the turn. The returning friend can play again on the next tide. A room refreshes while its tab is visible. Reloading the same tab restores your seat. The host can reopen the latest room with **Resume voyage with friends**. Guests can return through the same private invite link. Anyone with that link can play its seat.

After 90 seconds away from the table, the helm can pass to a connected friend. The new helm makes the public choices and can ask a bot to cover another sailor. Everyone keeps their own seat and hand.

After rescue or loss, the host or current helm can choose **Sail again with this crew**. Friends can join from the finished voyage and keep their seats for the next deal.

### The answering light

On tide six, the cutter’s lamp holds steady while a pale light moves against the swell. The boarding portraits show which sailors can mark the channel. Play Sounding line as the swimmer to mark it. The card gives one brace and costs one cold. On tide seven, the marked channel spares the hull and the swimmer takes two cold from exposure. Forcing a landing can cost up to four hull after brace. Scouting the reef costs four cold in all.

### The second wake

Hold the line to let brace absorb the pull, or follow the pull and take extra cold on the swimmer. Ring the Wreck bell from the raft to open **Haul in on the echo**. It spares the hull and reduces the swimmer’s exposure from two cold to one. The sailor who rings the bell pays the card’s cost. The swimmer recovers one ration on each route.

### The bell buoy

Brace to feel the chain. Two knocks before the bell mark the true chain. Follow it to recover three rations and take one extra cold on the swimmer. If the bell comes first, the swimmer recovers one ration and takes one extra cold. Breaking away lets brace absorb the hull damage and gives the swimmer normal exposure and recovery.

Saved voyages on versions 7 through 11 recover two rations on the true chain. Versions 7 through 16 take two extra cold when the bell comes first.

### Something under the raft

Use Sounding line from the water and Boathook from the raft to pry the creature loose. Cutting a timber costs two hull even with brace. Hauling the swimmer through adds one cold with brace or two cold without it. The creature follows the raft toward the reef after either fallback.

In versions 8 through 15, cutting costs one hull. In versions 8 through 12, hauling always adds two cold. Versions 13 and 14 require two brace to reduce that cost. The creature starts pursuing the raft from version 10.

### The towline

Let the rope drag and let brace absorb up to two hull damage, or slip the line to spare the hull and add one cold on the swimmer. In versions through 13, the swimmer’s route adds two cold.
